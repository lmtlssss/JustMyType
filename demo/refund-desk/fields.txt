Amounts are integer US cents. captured_cents is the original full payment including tax and shipping. A null value is unknown. prior_shipping_refunded is a boolean: when true, shipping_cents was already paid back. wrong_fulfillment is merchant-confirmed. Each line identifies one item, purchased, returned and accepted quantities, net paid and tax per unit, earlier settled and pending refunds for that returned line, final-sale and confirmed-damage flags, digital activation, and purchase/delivery/receipt dates. requested_date is the date of this request. For physical items received_date determines the return window; for digital items requested_date determines it. No source data is to be modified by planning.
